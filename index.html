IPS to Text Converter

Turn .ips ROM patches or Apple crash reports into readable .txt files. Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

ROM patches show every record: the ROM offset, the size, and the bytes written (hex + ASCII). RLE records show the fill value and count. Apple crash reports are pretty-printed as JSON. Tip: you can paste the output into a local AI (LM Studio, etc.) and ask it to explain the changes.
